Lake Tahoe

A page about my favorite place

Lake Tahoe is locatetd on the border of California and Nevada, higher up in the Sierra Nevada mountains...

The water is so clear that you can see dozens of feet down

Three spots worth the drive

  • Emerald Bay: the classic view, with a tiny island in the middle.
  • Rubicon Peak: a strenuous hike with a beautiful overhead view at the top.
  • Tahoe City: a quaint city with great food and local shopping.
